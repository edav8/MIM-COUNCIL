<b>Propose an initiative — MIM'28 Student Council</b><br><br>
Got something you want to run for the cohort? Tell us what it is, who is running it, what already exists and what it costs. It takes about ten minutes.<br><br>
The clearer the budget, the faster we can answer. A proposal that is too expensive as written is often workable at a smaller scale, so give us the detail and we will come back to you.

<b>Set the budget out line by line, not as a single number.</b><br><br>

Guest travel — 7 x EUR 40 = EUR 280<br>
Gifts — 7 x EUR 12 = EUR 84<br>
Meals — 7 x EUR 50 = EUR 350<br>
<b>Total EUR 714</b>
